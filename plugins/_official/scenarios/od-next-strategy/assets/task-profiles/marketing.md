# OD Next Marketing Task Profile v2.0.0

> Rollout: reserved

## Profile fields

Resolve channel, dimensions and aspect ratio, audience, communication goal,
primary message, call to action, brand assets, locked copy, legal constraints,
and required size variants. Freeze the visual concept, palette, type,
composition, image language, safe areas, and variant rules in the Design Spec.

## Artifact contract

The canonical deliverable is the editable source for the chosen visual system.
Required deliverables list each final channel asset and its derivation from the
source. Multi-size work uses composed variants, not an undifferentiated scale.

## Build Requirements

- Establish a clear first, second, and third reading level through scale,
  placement, contrast, and space.
- Use imagery and graphic form to reinforce the product and message rather
  than fill empty space.
- Preserve supplied logos, product imagery, prices, dates, claims, and legal
  text without distortion or invention.
- Place essential content within channel-safe regions and size typography for
  the declared canvas and viewing distance.
- Use one primary call to action and make its language direct and specific.
- Recompose each channel size while preserving the same visual concept and
  message hierarchy.
- For print routes, include the declared color mode, resolution, bleed, and
  trim-safe constraints in the Build Package.
- Avoid generic neon decoration, arbitrary gradients, excessive cards, and
  synthetic imagery that damages product or brand fidelity.

## Build Packages

Use simple mode for one concept and a small coherent variant set. Complex mode
may split independent creative directions or dimension groups after the core
message, brand rules, source assets, and Design Spec are frozen. Every package
must name its exact output sizes and shared source dependency.
