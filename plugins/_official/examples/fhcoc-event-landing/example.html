Oktoberfest

Three days of Bavarian food, Arizona brewers, and live music on the great lawn at Fountain Park. The chamber has run this festival since 1988, and every ticket keeps a local business on the map. Bring the family, the folding chairs, and the dachshund.

What's on

Three days built around the fountain

Everything sits inside the park, so you can walk from the biergarten to the family zone in about two minutes. Here is what draws the biggest crowds.

Biergarten and live music

Twelve Arizona taprooms pour under the big tent while brass bands, polka trios, and a Saturday night headliner trade off on the lawn stage. Steins are refillable all weekend.

Daily, opens with the gates

Family zone

A fenced play field with a carousel, a pretzel decorating table, face painting, and the lawn games tent. Staffed by chamber volunteers, and every activity inside the zone is included with admission.

Saturday and Sunday, noon to 5 p.m.

Dachshund dash

The town's favorite 30 yard sprint. Heats run Saturday afternoon, finals close out Sunday, and the winner takes home the copper stein plus a year of grooming from a member shop. Entry is $10 per dog.

Saturday heats, Sunday finals
