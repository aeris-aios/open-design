Where Business, Community, and Opportunity Flow

A letter from the chamber

2026 was the year Fountain Hills got busier. Our members opened 41 new storefronts along the Avenue and Saguaro Boulevard, and the chamber grew to 1,050 businesses. Every one of them showed up for each other, at ribbon cuttings, at mixers, and on the Oktoberfest grounds.

We spent the year on the work members told us matters most, which is getting found and getting introduced. The rebuilt online directory sent 31,000 visits to member listings, and 268 one-on-one advising sessions helped owners price, hire, and plan.

Thank you to the volunteers, the sponsors, and the board who carried this. The pages that follow are your year, in numbers. We are already building the 2027 calendar, and we would like to see your name on it.

Betsy Lawson
Board Chair
Marco Alvarez
