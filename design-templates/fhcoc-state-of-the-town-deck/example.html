What it means

Occupancy has not been this tight since 2018. Four of the five vacancies on the Avenue are already under lease, and two of those tenants relocated from Scottsdale to be closer to the fountain.

Tenure is the quieter story. A town where the average business has been open more than nine years is a town where owners are choosing to stay, and that stability is what keeps hiring steady through a slow summer.

Watch item for 2027: three long-standing owners plan to retire, so succession support moves onto the chamber program calendar in June.

Fountain Hills Chamber of Commerce 04 / 08

How the chamber spends its year

Three community pillars

Small business

Free one-on-one counseling, a monthly bookkeeping clinic, and a lending desk that helped 22 owners secure working capital last year. Members who use counseling renew at 99 percent.

1,050 members served

Tourism

The visitor center, the events calendar, and the shared regional campaign that puts Fountain Hills in front of Phoenix travelers. Every dollar of chamber promotion returned about eleven dollars in visitor spend.

$41M visitor spend

Workforce

A hiring board for local employers and a high school internship pipeline with the Fountain Hills Unified District. Thirty-one students finished a placement and nine were hired on.

236 jobs added
